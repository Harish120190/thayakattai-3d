# GAME RULES — THAYA NETRIK (தாய நேத்ரிக்)
## Indian Traditional Thayakattai Rules Engine & Regional Variations

### 1. Overview
**Thayakattai** (தாயக்கட்டை) is an ancient Tamil strategy board game belonging to the historic family of Indian cross-and-circle games (including Chaupar, Pachisi, and Pagade). 

Thaya Netrik faithfully implements the authentic traditional mechanics while offering configurable rule options to accommodate regional and household variations.

---

### 2. The Traditional Dice (Thayakattai / Paasi)
Traditional Thayakattai is played with a pair of long cuboidal brass or hardwood dice with 4 elongated faces:
- Faces are typically inscribed with: `1`, `2`, `3`, and `0` (blank).
- In some regional sets, dots represent `1`, `2`, `3`, and `4`.

#### Throw Outcomes (2 Long Dice):
| Die 1 | Die 2 | Total Throw | Traditional Name | Meaning / Effect | Bonus Roll? |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | 0 | **1** | **Thayam (தாயம்)** | Essential entry roll; unlocks new piece | **YES** |
| 0 | 1 | **1** | **Thayam (தாயம்)** | Essential entry roll; unlocks new piece | **YES** |
| 1 | 1 | **2** | **Irandu (இரண்டு)** | Move piece 2 spaces | No |
| 1 | 2 / 2, 1 | **3** | **Moondru (மூன்று)** | Move piece 3 spaces | No |
| 2 | 2 | **4** | **Naangu (நான்கு)** | Move piece 4 spaces | No |
| 2 | 3 / 3, 2 | **5** | **Ainthu (ஐந்து)** | Move piece 5 spaces | **YES** |
| 3 | 3 | **6** | **Aaru (ஆறு)** | Move piece 6 spaces | **YES** |
| 0 | 0 | **12** | **Panirendu (பன்னிரண்டு)** | Leap forward 12 spaces | **YES** |

#### Bonus Rolls (Extra Turns):
- A roll of **1 (Thayam)**, **5**, **6**, or **12** awards an immediate extra throw!
- Making a **Capture ("Vettu")** also awards an immediate extra throw!
- If multiple bonus rolls occur consecutively, all values are queued, and the player can allocate moves strategically across their active pieces on the board.

---

### 3. Token Entry ("Kottam" to Board)
- Each player starts with tokens in their **Home Sanctuary (Kottam)**.
- A token **cannot move** onto the board until the player rolls a **Thayam (1)**.
- Upon rolling a Thayam, the player may summon 1 token from Kottam onto their designated **Starting Safe Square (Starting Malai)**.
- Once entered, tokens advance along the circuit according to subsequent dice rolls.

---

### 4. Board Geometry & Paths
The game board consists of concentric tracks:
1. **Outer Perimeter Track**: 
   - Square circuit enclosing the board.
   - Tokens travel along this track (typically counter-clockwise or clockwise).
2. **Inner Sanctuary Track (Homeward Path)**:
   - Leads from the player's return gate into the inner sanctum.
3. **The Center (Pazham / Mahameru)**:
   - The central victory goal. Tokens that reach the exact center are crowned **Pazham** ("Ripe Fruit") and successfully finish their journey.

---

### 5. Safe Squares (Malai / Kallu)
- Key locations on the board are marked with an ornamental **X** (Cross):
  - Starting safe squares at the midpoint of each player's home perimeter edge.
  - Intermediate corner havens and junction forts.
- **Rules on Safe Squares**:
  - Tokens resting on a Malai are **completely immune to capture**.
  - Multiple tokens (belonging to different players or the same player) can peacefully coexist on a single safe square.

---

### 6. Captures (Vettu / Cut)
- When a token lands on a non-safe cell occupied by one or more opponent tokens:
  - The opponent's token is **captured** ("Vettu").
  - The captured token is sent back to the opponent's starting Kottam (must roll another Thayam to re-enter).
  - The capturing player receives an **Extra Roll** bonus!

#### The Essential "Vettu" Qualification Rule:
- In authentic Tamil Thayakattai, a player **MUST execute at least one capture ("Vettu")** during the game before ANY of their tokens are allowed to turn into the inner homeward path!
- If a player completes the outer perimeter without having made a capture, their tokens must continue circulating the outer track until the player achieves a capture.
- *Configurable variation*: In casual mode or beginner mode, this requirement can be toggled off.

---

### 7. Player Count Variations
Thaya Netrik supports:
- **2 Players**: North vs South starting bases, symmetric dual circuit paths.
- **4 Players**: South, East, North, West cross-mandala layout.
- **6 Players**: Hexagonal mandala with 6 player tracks, 6 colors, and symmetric forts.
- **8 Players**: Octagonal mandala with 8 player tracks and radial paths to the center.

---

### 8. Victory Conditions
1. The first player to bring all their tokens into the central **Pazham** wins **1st Place**.
2. Subsequent players can continue competing for 2nd and 3rd places, or match rankings are computed based on tokens completed and cell advancement.
