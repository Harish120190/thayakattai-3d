📜 Chola Dynasty Thayakattai (சோழர் தாயக்கட்டை)

Historical Context: In the golden age of the Imperial Chola Dynasty (10th–12th century CE), under Emperor Raja Raja Chola I and Rajendra Chola I, Thayakattai (தாயக்கட்டை) was played in royal temple mandapams and palace pavilions. The board is carved from royal teak and rosewood, inlaid with ivory tracks, jasper red safe havens, and crowned by a solid lathe-turned brass summit boss.

1. The Dice (பகடை / தாயக்கட்டை)

You can toggle between two authentic traditional styles:

  • Chola Hardwood Cubes: Two solid turned teakwood dice with circular brass-inlaid ring pips (as shown on the board). Rolling 1-1 (Thayam) or 6-6 (Dayam 12) unlocks tokens from the royal tray and grants bonus rolls!
  • Traditional Brass Sticks: Elongated 4-sided sticks with faces (0/Blank, 1, 2, 3) yielding traditional outcomes: Thayam (1), 2, 3, 4, Anju (5), Aaru (6), and Dayam (12).

2. Spawning Royal Medallion Coins

Tokens rest in the carved wooden trays (Left: Chola Gold Tigers, Right: Chola Ruby Warriors). Rolling a 1 (Thayam) or 12 (Dayam) allows you to march a coin from your tray onto the starting red square.

3. Safe Havens (மலை / Red Inlaid Squares)

All deep red jasper inlaid squares (corners, center cross, and bottom terminal) are sacred sanctuaries. Tokens resting on red squares cannot be captured!

4. Captures (Vettu / வெட்டு)

Landing on an opponent's coin on any standard ivory square captures it (Vettu!). The defeated coin is knocked back to its wooden tray, and the attacker receives an Immediate Extra Roll!

5. Ascending the Gopuram Staircase to the Summit

After navigating the outer circuit, coins enter the stepped temple gopuram staircase ascending towards the center column. The first ruler to guide all 4 royal coins to the sacred Summit Brass Boss achieves TRIUMPH!

6. 3D Camera Controls

  • Left Click + Drag: Freely rotate and inspect the hand-carved relief 360°
  • Mouse Wheel: Zoom in to inspect the engraved Chola Tiger coins and brass dice pips
  • Right Click + Drag: Pan camera across the royal Persian carpet
  • Preset Buttons: Instant switch between Photo Top-Down, Chola 3D, Dice Cam, and Coin Trays
